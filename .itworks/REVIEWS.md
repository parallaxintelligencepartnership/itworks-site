- entry id modulo bias and collision overwrite: the modulo is unbiased and the insert retries against the PRIMARY KEY
- badge colour arithmetic timezone dependent: dates are UTC truncated; the 29 and 30 day boundary held in every zone tried
- validation gaps beyond B1 and B2: unknown field, oversize, float for int, impossible date, future date, control character, inconsistent counts and wrong-case enums all rejected
- IPs stored: none stored; they were logged only, which is recorded above
- backup never left pi3: kopia covers /var/lib/docker/volumes nightly to B2 (knowledge base DR-COVERAGE.md); only the copy method and the missing restore were real
