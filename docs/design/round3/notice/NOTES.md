# THE PUBLIC NOTICE

1. The idea: the site is a bill wheat pasted on a civic board, so the page opens with a notice number and a date line, and the claim is posted in oversized condensed type on oat stock.
2. The badge is not a mark on the page, it is the notice, printed at its contract size and pinned at the top under the line "the notice, as posted".
3. Board A is a departure board: the state word sits in split flap cells that click over once on load, so a change of state reads as a mechanical event rather than a color swap.
4. Board B is the posted term: a 60 day rule with green and amber printed as zones, day 30 at the exact midpoint of the lane, and the red override stated flat underneath it.
5. The two install lines are tear off tabs under a perforated rule, which is what a fly posted bill does with the part you are meant to take away with you.
6. The empty wall is a departure board with every flap blank plus slot 001 ruled off with four pins in, so an empty register reads as a board waiting, never as an error.
7. Un SaaS by construction: no dark field, no glow, no cards, no bento, no gradient, no radius anywhere in the stylesheet, no hover lift, no numbered how it works strip, and no hero with a button.
8. Un SaaS by palette: printed ink on stock, the acid used as a misregistered second pass on the wordmark and as a highlighter block behind the commands, red held back for an open critical.
9. Type does the work: Archivo from wdth 80 to 118 for every structural line, JetBrains Mono only for machine facts, dates, counts and commands, never as a decorative label.
10. If it had to be simpler: cut the flap animation and the torn paste edge first, then reduce Board B to two plates and one sentence; the notice header, the register and the wall carry the idea alone.
