1. THE INSPECTION SHEET: the page is not about a record, it is the stamped inspection card itself, printed as form IW-01 with numbered boxes, serials, ruled entries, a punched binding edge and a signature block.
2. The badge is presented as what it already is on a card in a window, a rubber stamp: PASSED, STALE, CRITICAL, inked and rotated, set directly above the real flat contract badge so the impression and the artifact can be checked against each other.
3. The ruler of days is a printed engineer's scale, and the audit date is a punch through the card. The punch never moves, the day does, and it crosses the red 30 mark on its own; an open critical overrides the scale on day one.
4. The wall is a card file: serialed slots, a file tab per card, one ruled row of true columns, a mini punch scale under each card, and the tab pulls sideways out of the drawer on hover or focus.
5. Empty is a drawer standing open at slot IW-0001, ruled, serialed, unpunched, marked NO STAMP ISSUED, with the two install lines printed inside the blank card and slots 0002 and up waiting below it.
6. Un-SaaS one: the ground is cool card stock with printed non repro blue rules and punched holes showing the desk through them. No dark field, no glow, no gradient, no glass, no bento, no hover lift, no hero plus tagline plus CTA, no feature grid, no step strip.
7. Un-SaaS two: the only colors that are not ink are contract colors. Red, green and file blue come off a stamp pad, and the three badge plate hexes are printed beside the plates so a reader can verify them rather than admire them.
8. Un-SaaS three: the copy is form labels and field entries, not marketing prose, so the density is procedural. A reader scans boxes the way they scan an inspection card, and the page is short because a form is short.
9. Motion is one ink settle as a box enters view and one stamp press, transform and opacity only, driven by scroll position with no JavaScript, and prefers-reduced-motion switches it off with nothing lost.
10. If it had to be simpler: cut the ink displacement filter, the rail punch holes and the canary carbon copy footer. Keep the scale, the stamp and the ruled columns, and the sheet still reads as a record rather than a page.
